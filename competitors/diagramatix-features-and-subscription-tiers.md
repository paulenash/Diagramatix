# Diagramatix — Features & Subscription Tiers

This document lists every feature published on the Diagramatix **Features** page, followed by a
**feature‑availability table by subscription tier** (Introductory · Professional · Expert · Enterprise).

Every paid tier includes the **full core diagramming, AI, import/export and collaboration toolset** listed
in Part 1. The tier table in Part 2 shows only what **differs** between tiers — the premium analysis
modules, the enterprise capabilities, and the usage limits.

---

## Part 1 — All Features

### Diagramming & Editing

- **Multi‑Notation Diagramming** — One workspace for every diagram type your team needs — from BPMN to ArchiMate to UML.
- **Cross‑Functional Flowcharts (Pools / Lanes / Sub‑Lanes)** — Model your organisation as it really works — multi‑pool, multi‑lane, multi‑sub‑lane.
- **Smart Connector Routing** — Connectors that route cleanly around obstacles — no manual zig‑zagging.
- **Drag‑Drop Palette + Smart Editing UX** — Built for fast iteration — drag, drop, type, done.
- **Properties Panel with Per‑Element Configuration** — Click any element and edit every property without leaving the canvas.
- **Reusable Templates with Groups** — Save your common patterns once; reuse them across every project.
- **Drill‑Down Navigation** — Link diagrams together for true hierarchical process documentation.
- **Custom Display Modes (Normal + Hand‑Drawn)** — Switch any diagram between polished and sketchy with one click.
- **Configurable Colour Themes per Project & per Diagram** — Match your brand or process taxonomy with custom colours.
- **Diagram Title Block with Version / Authors / Status** — Professional title block stamp for every diagram.
- **Diagram‑Type Colour Identity** — Every diagram type gets a 2‑character code and a distinct pastel colour, so you can tell process types apart at a glance.

### AI & Voice

- **AI‑Assisted BPMN Generation** — Describe the process in plain English; Diagramatix builds a structured BPMN diagram you can refine.
- **Text‑to‑Diagram for Every Notation** — AI generation isn't BPMN‑only — describe it in plain English and generate Value Chain, Process Context, State Machine, Domain, Context and ArchiMate diagrams too.
- **Image to Diagram** — Snap a whiteboard sketch, a screenshot, or someone else's flowchart and Diagramatix reverse‑engineers it into an editable BPMN or flowchart.
- **Voice Dictation (Speech‑to‑Text)** — Describe your process out loud — high‑accuracy voice dictation feeds straight into AI Generate for every diagram type, so you can talk a diagram into existence instead of typing.

### Analysis & Quality

- **Process Simulator** — Run your BPMN as a discrete‑event simulation — see where work queues, who the bottleneck is, and prove the cost case for a redesign before you change anything.
- **Diagram Health Check & Connector Scan** — Scan a whole project for BPMN connector and structure problems — especially handy after a Visio import — and fix them one at a time, each highlighted on its own diagram.
- **Automatic Diagram Linking** — Let Diagramatix wire up the sub‑process links across a project for you — match each sub‑process to the diagram that details it by name or process number, then confirm in one dialog.
- **Flowchart → BPMN Translation** — Modernise legacy Standard Flowcharts into BPMN — translate an existing flowchart diagram into a proper BPMN process map in one step, so teams can move off the old flowchart standard onto the current business‑process‑modelling standard, BPMN.

### Import, Export & Interoperability

- **Microsoft Visio Round‑Trip** — Author in Diagramatix, share with Visio users, get edits back — no loss in translation.
- **BPMN 2.0 XML Import** — Bring diagrams in from any BPMN‑compliant tool.
- **Bulk Visio Export** — Export every BPMN diagram in a project as one multi‑page `.vsdx` file.
- **SharePoint & OneDrive Integration** — Sign in with Microsoft, save and open diagrams in SharePoint or OneDrive, and link Data Objects to live documents.
- **Backup & Restore** — One‑click full‑account snapshot to a portable `.diag` file.
- **Guided Backups with Live Progress** — See exactly what will be backed up, choose who to include, and watch it happen — with a report at the end.

### Organisation, Collaboration & Governance

- **Project & Folder Organisation** — Group diagrams by project, organise within folders, sort however you like.
- **Project Sharing with Roles** — Share a project with the people who need it — View or Edit, per project, anytime.
- **Collaboration & Diagram Review** — Circulate a diagram to colleagues, gather comments, and track sign‑off — all inside Diagramatix.
- **Organisation Admin & Settings** — Designate organisation administrators with project‑share oversight, configurable per‑organisation sharing policies, and silent admin membership.
- **Entity Lists — Governed Pool & Lane Naming** — Name BPMN pools and lanes from a maintained Organisation hierarchy, external‑participant list and IT‑systems list — consistent across every diagram.
- **Publishing & Review Lifecycle** — Publish a versioned diagram — or a whole bundle of related diagrams — to a business‑user audience, with scheduled re‑review reminders so process maps never silently go stale.
- **Tiered Subscriptions with Self‑Serve Upgrade** — Start free, upgrade when you need more — at any time.

### Notes

- A **Free** tier is also available for evaluation (1 project, 1 diagram per type, capped elements, 5 AI
  attempts, 30‑day trial). It is omitted from the table above, which covers the paid tiers.
- **Expert** is the current top self‑serve tier — it unlocks all four premium analysis modules (Simulator,
  Process Mining, Risk & Control + Compliance, APQC) with unlimited projects and diagrams.
- **Enterprise** is the planned top tier for organisations: everything in Expert **plus** live co‑authoring
  (real‑time cursors, live‑edit ghosts, merge‑on‑Sync) and Enterprise Mode (single sign‑on / SCIM
  provisioning, per‑organisation AI & voice policies, and single‑tenant deployment). Pricing and the exact
  entitlement set are finalised per engagement.
- Premium‑module access is allocated per tier by a SuperAdmin in the Subscriptions editor; the figures above
  reflect the current configuration.
