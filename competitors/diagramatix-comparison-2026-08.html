Executive summary

Diagramatix is a full-loop process, governance and documentation platform for the SMB and mid-market: classify against the APQC standard, author in any notation with the LLM of your choice, publish, mine the real process, check conformance, calibrate a digital twin, simulate the redesign, govern it with a Risk-Control Matrix, monitor whether controls keep operating org-wide — and now generate the SOPs straight from the model — without leaving the tool.

  • ◆ Diagram-grounded AI SOP generation. Turn a whole process — or a single lane (a role SOP with both-direction hand-offs), pool, subprocess or linked group — into an editable Word procedure that always matches the model, adopts your org's Word template styles, and regenerates non-destructively (your edits, added and locked sections survive). PRIME's AI Procedure Writer works from recordings; Diagramatix's works from the truth — the diagram — so it never drifts from what you published.
  • ◆ Bring-your-own-AI, cost-governed. Generation runs on any of Claude, Kimi/Moonshot, Google Gemini or Microsoft Azure OpenAI — admin-selectable, self-hosted key or gateway — with per-org usage metering, token-cost rates and an "allow AI" governance switch (plus optional pseudonymisation before a prompt leaves the tenant). The enterprise suites tie you to one vendor's AI with no per-org cost lens.
  • ◆ A closed mine → conform → simulate → govern → document loop on one product. Most tools do either mining or modelling + simulation; Diagramatix does both and wires mining output straight into a calibrated twin, proves each control's operating effectiveness from the mined conformance, trends it org-wide over time, and writes the procedures — a workflow the enterprise suites keep in separate modules.
  • ◆ APQC PCF as a living, org-owned framework. Classify against it, generate running models from it, measure coverage and roll control-effectiveness up by APQC category, and compose / version / divisionalise your own tailored framework with provenance and a stable-id upgrade wizard — not just a browsable library.
  • ◆ Text-, image- and voice-to-diagram across every notation (incl. ArchiMate with a custom icon library), Microsoft Visio round-trip, BPSim interchange, 40+ live diagram-health scan rules, and automatic sub-process linking — a self-service experience the enterprise suites don't emphasise.
Diagramatix

The deepest enterprise-architecture platform: scale, repository governance, mining, the most mature simulation, risk/compliance. Diagramatix leads on self-service, provider-choice AI/image/voice generation, Visio round-trip, the mining-to-twin loop and model-grounded SOPs.

PRIME BPM

Closest in the business-user improvement segment (BPMN mapping, APQC library, RACI, AI Procedure Writer, approvals). Diagramatix leads on mining, conformance, digital-twin simulation, notation breadth, Visio round-trip, provider-choice AI, and turns the APQC library into a living framework; its SOP generator is diagram-grounded rather than recording-driven.

Bottom line

Diagramatix now classifies, models, publishes, mines, governs, simulates, monitors and documents processes as one self-hosted workflow — on the AI you choose, with the cost metered. Real-time co-authoring has now shipped too — live presence + cursors, soft element locks, and version-guarded saves with automatic conflict-merge. The honest remaining gaps versus Signavio / ARIS / Celonis are enterprise-scale mining connectors, a general org-wide process-intelligence dashboard, a configurable approval-workflow engine + full audit, and deep repository governance.

Where we stand

Where Diagramatix leads

  1. Model-grounded AI SOPs. Procedures generated from the diagram (whole or a single role's lane, with hand-offs), house-branded to your Word template, and regenerated non-destructively as the model changes — always in sync with what's published.
  2. Bring-your-own, cost-governed AI. Choose Claude, Kimi, Gemini or Microsoft; self-hosted or gateway; per-org usage metering, token costs and an allow-AI switch. No suite offers provider choice with a per-org cost lens.
  3. Mining-proven control effectiveness on one model, trended org-wide over time — the suites keep GRC and mining in separate products.
  4. APQC as a living, org-owned framework — classify, generate-from, coverage, by-category compliance, tailor + version + upgrade — not a browsable library.
  5. The integrated loop — mining feeds a calibrated twin in one click; discovery, conformance, simulation and documentation aren't separate modules.
  6. Input breadth + self-service — text, image and voice to diagram across every notation; Visio round-trip both ways; BPSim; free tier + self-serve upgrade; self-hosted.
  7. Real-time co-authoring, self-hosted. Live presence + cursors, soft element locks, and a version guard with automatic 3-way merge (concurrent edits to different shapes merge silently; only a same-shape clash is flagged) — the enterprise suites' concurrent-editing advantage, in a per-seat self-hosted tool.

Where we still trail (honest gaps)

  1. Enterprise mining at scale. We mine uploaded logs (incl. OCEL); Celonis / Signavio PI / ARIS extract live from source systems with pre-built connectors at scale.
  2. Repository governance. ARIS/Signavio offer deeper repository objects, glossaries and lifecycle governance, plus a configurable approval-workflow engine + full audit log.
  3. General org-wide process intelligence. Compliance is monitored org-wide; a general live KPI dashboard (throughput, cycle-time, cost across the org) is still not present.
